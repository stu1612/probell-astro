# Probell Nutrition — Brand

Read this for copy, imagery or any new section or page. It describes
intent. Use judgement within it and propose ideas.

## What the site is

Probell is an American-directed supplement brand built around strength,
performance and blue-collar identity. The kettlebell-shaped protein
container is the product. The brand is the person who picks it up.

The site is a **brand presentation and business development tool**, not
an ecommerce store, marketplace or blog. It has one goal: **get the right
people to make contact.** Those people are gyms, distributors, retailers
and sales partners. Consumer appeal matters because it makes the B2B case:
a gym owner has to believe their members will want this.

## Market and go-to-market

- **US market only.** An earlier Sweden-first plan was dropped.
- **Phase 1, validation:** a first run of ~300–400 units and a premium
  booth at the Arnold Sports Festival, aiming for qualified leads rather
  than sales. Then in-person visits to California gyms and retailers. The
  Arnold Expo date is still unconfirmed with the client, and it's the one
  hard external deadline.
- **Phase 2, sales network:**
  - Who: independent, commission-based Sales Partners.
  - Where: California, Texas, Florida, Arizona, Nevada and New York.
  - Requirements: fluent English, based in the US, gym or retail sales
    experience.
  - The Sales Partner page supports this.

## Partner pages

Each page has one honest stance. Never invent traction, scale or numbers
(the production run size stays private).

- **Sales Partner (`/sales`):** qualifies on belief, not credentials. No
  experience or resume fields. "Why Probell?" is the qualifying input.
  Region and event are free text: there are no fixed territories yet, and
  free text surfaces opportunities a dropdown would miss. No published
  commission terms.
- **Retailer (`/retail`):** small scale is a strength ("someone who won't
  waste your time"). Wholesale pricing is shared on request, never
  published, and the page should make that feel like normal B2B practice.
  No business-type dropdown or location field; those belong in the
  follow-up conversation.
- **Distributor (`/distributor`):** aimed at regional or mid-size
  distributors scouting new brands, not enterprise. Deliberately minimal:
  intro, one short paragraph, and a form with no message field. No stat
  chips, FAQ or automation. Over-building it would look like compensating
  for a lack of track record.

## Principles

1. **Earned, not aspirational.** The brand is for people who already show
   up. Copy and images should feel like recognition, not motivation.
2. **The product is the identity.** The kettlebell container belongs in a
   gym. It's always present and never secondary.
3. **Americana without parody.** The flag wrap, worn textures and
   blue-collar tone are genuine. Don't exaggerate them.
4. **Confidence over hype.** No countdowns, urgency or fitness clichés.
   Disciplined, direct and certain.
5. **Image-led, text-supported.** Photography does the emotional work and
   copy confirms it.
6. **Instant recognition.** A visitor knows what Probell is within three
   seconds.
7. **Density over whitespace.** The site should feel full without feeling
   cluttered. Use negative space for contrast, not by default.

## Voice

| Do | Avoid |
|---|---|
| "Fuel for people who already show up." | "Unleash your potential" |
| "No shortcuts. No fillers. No compromise." | "Premium quality formula" |
| "The only protein you can actually lift." | "Take your fitness to the next level" |
| "Built for the grind." | "Advanced scientifically-backed formula" |
| "Stock Probell. Be first." | "Join our community" |

Short, direct and certain. No fluff, no explanation, no apology.

## Photography

- **Grade:** cinematic. Deep crushed blacks, warm amber highlights, high
  contrast, desaturated, hard directional light, no bright whites.
- **Setting:** hardcore gyms. Concrete or brick, chalk dust, iron plates,
  power racks, industrial lighting. Never clean or commercial gyms.
- **Athletes:** mid- or post-effort, real physiques. Not posing, not
  smiling, not looking at the camera.
- **Product:** always the Americana flag wrap, always shown in context.
  Never isolated on white.
- **Never:** a stock-photo look, soft or pastel light, a wellness
  aesthetic, or anything that could belong to another brand.
- **Logo:** use the PNG asset only. Never recreate it in CSS or type.

## Avoid

- Anything that reads as ecommerce, wellness-soft or SaaS.
- Decorative or storytelling animation.
- Large empty sections and decorative typography.
- Layouts that could belong to any other brand.

## Success looks like

- The brand is understood in under three seconds.
- A gym owner or distributor feels compelled to make contact.
- The site feels built, not templated, and only Probell could own it.
